Concrescence

In the beginning, there was only Habit. A universe of sameness, repeating endlessly. But within the stillness, a flicker of Potential resides, waiting to be awakened.

About the Game

This game is a small interactive simulation inspired by the ideas of Terence McKenna's Novelty Theory. It explores the cosmic struggle between Habit, the tendency towards repetition, and Novelty, the emergence of new and complex forms.
